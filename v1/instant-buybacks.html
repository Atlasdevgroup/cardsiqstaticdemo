Common questions.

Do we have to buy back every hit?
No. You set eligibility at the business level: graders (PSA, BGS, SGC), value range, and the buyback rate. CardsIQ carries those rules into every eligible offer, then handles verification and settlement.
Where does market value come from?
Market context from ALT and Card Ladder is attached to each card. The instant offer is the rate you set, applied to that ALT + Card Ladder market value.
What is checked before payout?
Identity, card, account, and duplicate checks run against the same transaction record. Settlement completes only after verification passes, and the record closes with the full history attached.
What happens after the stream ends?
Nothing needs to be rebuilt. The buyer, card, break context, offer, verification, and settlement stay on one record, so break activity connects to pricing and payouts without rebuilding the transaction after the stream.
