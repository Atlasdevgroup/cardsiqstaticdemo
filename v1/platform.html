Common questions.

CardsIQ is infrastructure. It works with what you run today.

Does CardsIQ replace our marketplace or break platform?
No. CardsIQ is infrastructure, not another destination. It connects the operations between the platforms you already use, so card and transaction context survives across inventory, marketplaces, live commerce, payments, and fulfillment.
How do cards get into CardsIQ?
Through structured intake from graded certification data (PSA, BGS, SGC), raw-card SKUs, imports, or integrated intake workflows. Each card gets an identity and an internal SKU before it moves.
Where does pricing come from?
Market context from ALT and Card Ladder is attached to each card, alongside your cost basis and pricing rules.
Do we have to buy back every hit?
No. You decide what you buy: eligible graders, value range, buyback rate, and duplicate and identity checks. CardsIQ carries those rules into every eligible offer, then handles verification and settlement.
Is CardsIQ only for businesses?
No. Collectors use CardsIQ to know what they own, with cards, offers, ownership, transactions, wallet, and break history in one place. Create account
