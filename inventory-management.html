Inventory questions.

Start with the cards you already hold, graded or raw.

Can we bring in the inventory we already have?
Yes. Cards enter through structured intake from graded certification data, raw-card SKUs, imports, or integrated intake workflows. Each card gets an identity and an internal SKU before it moves.
Does it handle raw cards as well as graded slabs?
Yes. Graded cards are matched from PSA, BGS, and SGC cert data. Raw cards enter with a raw intake record, their own SKU, and their source attached, then sit in the same directory as your slabs.
Where does market value come from?
Each card carries ALT + Card Ladder market data: fair market value and last sale from both sources, alongside your own cost basis.
What happens to card-level economics inside a product?
They stay attached. When you build a case or break product from live inventory, every card keeps its cert, cost basis, market value, and status, so the product's totals are the sum of the cards inside it.
