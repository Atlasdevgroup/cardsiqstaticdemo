Before you buy back your first hit.

How the live workspace and the buyback workflow fit together.

How do StreamIQ and Instant Buybacks work together?
StreamIQ connects the show, products, breaks, buyer assignments, and card hits. Instant Buybacks carries eligible hits into offers, verification, and settlement, keeping the same buyer, card, and transaction context. Explore StreamIQ
Do we have to buy back every hit?
No. You set eligibility at the business level: graders (PSA, BGS, SGC), value range, and the buyback rate, plus fee responsibility. CardsIQ carries those rules into every eligible offer, then handles verification and settlement.
Where does the reference price come from?
Market context from ALT and Card Ladder is attached to each card. The offer is the buyback rate you set, applied to that reference price, and each offer keeps the rate it was created with.
What is checked before payout?
Identity, card, account, and duplicate checks run against the same transaction record. Settlement completes only after verification passes, and the record closes with the full history attached, without rebuilding the transaction after the stream.
