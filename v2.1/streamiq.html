Questions

StreamIQ questions.

StreamIQ runs beside the platforms you already stream on.

WhatnotTikTok LIVEOBS program bridgeALT + Card Ladder

Will StreamIQ slow the show down?
No. The workspace is the source of truth for the stream. Spots, buyers, hits, and offers are captured as they happen, so nothing has to be re-keyed after the show.
How does StreamIQ connect to our production?
Through the OBS program bridge. StreamIQ bridges scenes and program output into the live workspace, alongside fills, buyer assignments, and card hits.
Does each break keep its own records?
Yes. One show can run multiple breaks, and every break keeps its own ledger. Each one remains independently auditable while still belonging to the larger show and production session.
What happens after a card is hit?
The hit is written to the break with its buyer already attached. StreamIQ turns hits into offers, fulfillment, and settled history, so eligible cards can become instant buybacks without rebuilding the transaction after the stream. See Instant Buybacks
