    Who is CardsIQ for?
    CardsIQ is built for breakers, card shops, repack companies, marketplaces, and show vendors who want to connect inventory, live commerce, offers, and card history in one system.
    Do I need to use every CardsIQ product?
    No. There's no generic package. Start where you need CardsIQ most, whether that's inventory, instant buybacks, live commerce, or licensed repacks, and we scope the modules that fit your operation.
    Can CardsIQ work with my existing workflow?
    Yes. CardsIQ is infrastructure, not another destination. It connects the operations between the platforms you already use: PSA, BGS, and SGC cert data, ALT and Card Ladder market data, Whatnot and TikTok LIVE, and raw-card intake from Heystack or a CSV import.
    What happens after I submit an inquiry?
    We route it to the right CardsIQ team, then scope the CardsIQ modules that fit your operation. We start with how your business actually works.
